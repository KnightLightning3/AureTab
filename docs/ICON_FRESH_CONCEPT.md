## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished square app icon for AureTab, a minimal browser new-tab extension. Fresh, quiet, contemporary, exceptionally simple and readable at tiny sizes. A single bespoke geometric capital A mark with softly rounded stroke ends, an open triangular counter and a short clean crossbar, with a subtly tab-like rounded upper apex. The A is solid fresh medium teal (#20A99E), with a small integrated sky-blue (#63B8EE) accent on the right descending stroke; only two flat colors, absolutely no gold or navy. Center the mark at about 60% of canvas width on a very pale cool mint-white rounded square tile with generous margins. Flat vector-like precision, balanced optical weight, confident thick strokes, crisp silhouette, generous negative space. Square front view, just the icon fills the image; no presentation board, mockup, perspective, captions, brand name, extra text, borders, shadows, texture, 3D, metallic finish, elaborate gradients, stars or decorative dots. The tile corners should be softly rounded, outside the tile transparent. This is a new visual direction, not a vintage monogram.
